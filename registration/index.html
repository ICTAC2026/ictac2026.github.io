Registration


🏷 Regular Conference Full Access: Covers attendance to all main conference sessions (11–13 November), school sessions (9–10 November), and Industry Day (14 November). Includes lunches and coffee breaks on 11–13 November, coffee breaks on 9–10 and 14 November, the conference banquet on 12 November, and a conference T-shirt.
🏷 Regular Conference + Industry Day: Covers attendance to all main conference sessions (11–13 November) and Industry Day (14 November). Includes lunches and coffee breaks on 11–13 November, coffee breaks on 14 November, the conference banquet on 12 November, and a conference T-shirt.
🏷 Post-Graduate School: Covers all the sessions of the school (9 and 10 November) and coffee breaks during 9-10 November.
🏷 Student registrations: Participants must be full-time students to be registered as students and must provide valid evidence of their full-time student status (e.g. a scan of their student card).
📢 Workshop Day: The Sunday Nov. 15 workshop is not included in general registration and requires a separate registration. You must register for the conference and the workshop individually. Please select the corresponding option during registration.
⚠ Cancellations are not possible. Thank you for your understanding.

For registration, please fill out the Registration Form.

📢 News: The registration is now closed! If you want to register please contact ictac2026@dc.exa.unrc.edu.ar.
